BANSHEE
OCTOBER 31
10PM TILL 8AM
(with an extra hour because the clock is set back)

BANSHEE is a HALLOWEEN CELEBRATION. It's an opportunity for all of us to gather and celebrate our weirdness and manifest our magical feats.

On October 31st, the BANSHEE will share her murky lair with us. Do not be alarmed by her supersonic shrieks, she is simply welcoming us into her dank crypt for the night. Join us in adding our own screams to the cacophony of her music. No black magic, positive vibes only! But of course you should dress as spooky as you want

This event will take place in an abandoned warehouse. We are breaking the law to dance motherfuckeeers! SO ~ it is every attendee’s responsibility to help make this party a success.
To avoid this party getting shut down we need to keep a low profile. Please read the guidelines laid out in the INFO & RULES section and follow our rules to reduce unwanted attention to the building and keep us free to dance till dawn.

BRING YOUR POWERS, LIGHTS AND BEST COSTUME FOR THE OCCASION.

🕯 🕯 🕯

We are looking for help for this rave! Sign up to volunteer here

tickets


Tickets come with an arrival time. We do not want 150 people arriving at the meetup spot at the same time. Please respect our timing policy and be aware of the time change between 1-2am.

NO ENTRY after 4 AM.

You can purchase your ticket here.

If you want FREE ACCESS, you can sign up as a volunteer here.

Feel free to contact us at
the.banshee.night@gmail.com
if the ticket price is prohibitively expensive for you, and we can work something out.

info


Do NOT share any information about this rave on social media. Only send this party invite to trusted friends.

The location you will receive is not the exact address of the party but a meeting spot nearby. You will be escorted to the venue in small groups by our chaperones.

rules


(1) Arrive at the meetup location; no one will be allowed inside without an escort.

(2) Once you are in, DO NOT share the location of the venue. Tell your friends to go to the meetup spot.

(3) If leaving/arriving by taxi/Uber, PLEASE use only the designated meetup spot for pickup/dropoff.

(4) NO RE-ENTRY; when you choose to leave, you will be escorted back to the meeting point.

(5) NO LOITERING outside the building EVEN AFTER LEAVING.

(6) This warehouse has inherent dangers. Please be careful inside especially if exploring beyond the bounds of the party

(7) Dress warmly and safely (and spooky!). Close-toed shoes and no heels. Prepare appropriately to be in an unheated building overnight, and potentially having to wait at the meeting spot (outdoors).

(8) PLUR in effect. Care for others as you would for yourself.

🕯 🕯 🕯

If you want FREE ACCESS, you can sign up as a volunteer here.

contact


the.banshee.night@gmail.com

This is community-driven event, and we welcome all contributions. Please contact us if you are able to contribute with a creative project, materials, or hands-on help :)
